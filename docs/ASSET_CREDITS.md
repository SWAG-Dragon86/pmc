# Artwork credits and licenses

Updated 2026-10-23 after the maintainer authorized the two shared Gourgeist portraits and confirmed original background authorship.

All 350 catalog form image files now have contributor permission records: 277 SpriteCollab files (198 full-body frames / 79 portraits) and 73 The Artificial icons. The two Gourgeist size forms share a species portrait with explicit maintainer approval; their separate configurations and calculations remain unchanged. The two Mega Meowstic image files share the Mega design; the previous two files were already byte-identical.

18 type icons retain James Watkins / partywhale MIT terms. 37 balls retain Cobblemon Assets CC BY-NC 3.0 terms. The maintainer removed the unverified Strange Ball; 37 ball themes remain. The default background is maintainer-confirmed original and retained unchanged; a standalone reuse license has not been selected.

Original code remains GPL-3.0-or-later. Contributor artwork permissions do not grant all underlying Pokemon character or trademark rights. Do not describe this as official IP clearance or apply the artwork’s noncommercial condition to GPL code. Historical SpriteCollab permissions and newer CC BY-NC 4.0 terms remain recorded separately.

The Artificial author statement says CC-BY and free reuse with credit to The Artificial. Its exact CC version is unspecified; see public/licenses/theartificial-permission.txt. It is not relabeled as CC-BY-4.0 or GPL.

Retain authors, source, license and modification notices when redistributing artwork or exported cards. Exported PNG metadata carries the relevant credits, but social platforms may strip metadata; retain attribution separately when that happens. See [remaining gaps](ASSET_RIGHTS_REMAINING.md).


The eight repository screenshots were recaptured from the prepared build and retain component attributions in PNG metadata. Previous public Git blobs still exist; this does not clear or erase historical distribution.
